Heat model (estimates, not measured): wall temperature 265 °C in the block, falling to 90 °C across the exposed heat break (Z 25.3 to 28.1), 90 to 55 °C up the heat break inside the heatsink, 50 °C in the heatsink above it and 40 °C above the heatsink. Each 0.25 mm of filament moves toward its local wall temperature with the time constant set above (3 s is a rough figure for 1.75 mm filament in a close bore). The start state is steady printing at 2.5 mm/s of filament.

Liquid: material above 200 °C cannot be pulled up as a solid. On a pull, the tip moves 6 % of the pull and the liquid column stretches and thins (volume kept). On a push, it compresses back; anything pushed past the nozzle tip is extruded. Thinning is drawn by narrowing the filament.

Timing: extruder moves are shown slowed down and waits are shown sped up; the label above the step title gives the factor. The heat model always runs on real time. Head travel times are estimates (marked in the steps).

Toolhead board: drawn as Voron Design's reference Stealthburner toolhead PCB; toolhead boards such as BTT's SB2209 mount in the same place. The board does not affect the analysis.

Moving parts: both extruder gears turn with every extruder move (hob contact radius 3.7 mm); the cutter arm is driven by the head motion (touch at the pin, then cut_move_dist further, at AFC's cut speeds) on its M3 pivot, blade holder sliding in X, the return spring (drawn as the parts-list spring: 0.5 × 4 × 25 mm, 14 coils, bent between the arm's pocket, tilted 18° up, and the level printhead pocket; compressed to about 13.1 mm at rest and 11.1 mm at full stroke, its arm end turning from 18° to 6°; it sits behind the cut plane inside the arm's pocket, so where the arm covers it, it is drawn faintly as a hidden part; the CAD models a straight 15 mm spring), guidler swinging in 0.15 mm when the gears are empty, switch balls riding on the filament.

Where these settings live

All AFC files are in your Klipper config folder (printer_data/config/), under AFC/. Edit them from Mainsail or Fluidd (Machine tab), save, then run RESTART.

retract_length, pushback_length, cut_move_dist
AFC/AFC_Macro_Vars.cfg, section [gcode_macro _AFC_CUT_TIP_VARS]: variable_retract_length, variable_pushback_length, variable_cut_move_dist. The pin location, cut speeds and rip_length are in the same section. If your config has a per-extruder _AFC_CUT_TIP_VARS_<extruder> macro, its values override these.
Try a value without editing the file
SET_GCODE_VARIABLE MACRO=_AFC_CUT_TIP_VARS VARIABLE=retract_length VALUE=6 takes effect at the next cut and lasts until the next restart. The variable name drops the variable_ prefix.
tool_stn_unload (the 100 mm unload)
AFC/AFC_Hardware.cfg, section [AFC_extruder extruder], with tool_unload_speed.
z_hop
AFC/AFC.cfg, section [AFC].
